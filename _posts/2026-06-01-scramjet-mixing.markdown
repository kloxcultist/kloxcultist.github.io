Conducting a non-reacting numerical analysis of a hydrogen-fueled scramjet combustor to explore different cavity geometries for mixing enhancement. The study provides quantitative evaluation using key performance parameters: mixing efficiency (MxE), total pressure recovery (TPR), mass-averaged Mach number (MAMN), and flammable zone coverage ratio (FZCR).
